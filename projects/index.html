Research

Understanding molecules, from the materials we make to the chemistry of life.

I use molecular simulation, quantum physics, and machine learning to connect the behaviour of atoms to larger questions: how can we design more sustainable polymers, what makes biological chemistry distinctive, and when do quantum effects change a reaction?

My current focus at the University of Birmingham is polymer properties and biodegradability. This builds on my work on chemical complexity and the origins of biochemistry at Arizona State University, and quantum biology at Surrey. Developing useful research software and reliable datasets runs through all three.
