I received a Best Poster prize at [Frontiers and Challenges in Quantum Biology](https://indico.nbi.ku.dk/event/2190/)
at the Niels Bohr Institute in Copenhagen. The poster, with Marco Sacchi, explored nuclear quantum effects in
guanine–thymine misincorporation in DNA polymerase λ.
