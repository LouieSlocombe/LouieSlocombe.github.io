Our new [preprint on proteome elemental composition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19333), with L. Felipe Benites and Sara Walker,
looks at the elements that make up proteins across cellular and viral life. We find striking consistency across thousands
of proteomes and explore what this might tell us about the origins of the amino acid alphabet.
