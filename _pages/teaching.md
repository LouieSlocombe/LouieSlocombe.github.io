Teaching is a regular part of my research life, from introducing undergraduates to scientific programming to helping
PhD students and postdocs with their calculations. I teach across physics and chemistry, with an emphasis on writing
code, working together, and using computational methods to answer research questions.

I've also helped develop teaching on generative AI and its use in programming. My Associate Fellowship of the
Higher Education Academy focused on generative AI in university teaching and learning.
