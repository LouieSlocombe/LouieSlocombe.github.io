Publications

Research in quantum biology, molecular simulation, chemical complexity, and machine learning.

These papers follow my work from quantum effects in DNA to chemical complexity and the origins of biochemistry. They include journal articles, conference contributions, and work still in progress, with preprints and manuscripts listed separately below. You can search by title, author, or topic.

For the story behind the papers, visit my research pages. Citation information is on Google Scholar, and my CV brings together the rest of my academic work.
